Type it. Get it

Aequalis answers maths, units, currencies and time as you type.

Write your request the way you would say it. If it can be read in more than one way, Aequalis asks which one you mean: no guessing.

Aequalis is available for Mac, Linux, Windows and Android (currently in the close testing phase) Installation instructions are available here:

Read the docs

2400 Thai currency: how much is it worth in dollars?

aequalis> 2400 Thailand usd

2400 THB = 71.09 USD

My shoe size is 44 in Italy: what is the corresponding US size?

aequalis> 44 it us

- shoes: 44 it = US 10 men, US 11.5 women

- clothes: 44 it = US 8 women, US 34 men's suit, US 28 men's trousers

It is 10:00 in Paris: what time is it in New York?

aequalis> 10:00AM Paris New York

10:00AM Paris to New York = 24 Oct 26 4:00 AM GMT-4 New York

Distance between Paris and New York?

aequalis> Paris New York

5,853 km

And the time now, in two cities?

aequalis> now Rome Tokyo

24 Oct 26 8:17 PM GMT+2 Rome

25 Oct 26 3:17 AM GMT+9 Tokyo

How fast is 100 km/h in miles per hour?

aequalis> 100 km/h mph

100 km/h = 62.1371 mph

What is 20% of 150?

aequalis> 20% of 150

20% of 150 = 30

How much fuel does 8 litres per 100 km burn, in miles per gallon?

aequalis> 8 l/100km mpg

8 l/100km = 29.4018 mpg

What you can type

Maths

12+4/2 gives 14, 2^10 gives 1024, 20% of 150 gives 30, and sqrt, log, sin, 5! are there too.

Units

100 km/h mph gives 62.1371 mph. Length, area, volume, mass, temperature, speed, pressure, energy and more.

Currencies

12 usd euro by code, name, symbol or country. Rates are synced once a day; add a date for a past day's rates: 100 usd eur 2025-01-30, yesterday, 3 months ago, -1wd (750 days back).

Dates and time

12 Sept 2026 + 5wd gives 18/09/2026. 10:00AM Paris to New York gives the time over there.

Calendar

cal shows this month; cal march UAE,USA,Italy marks the three countries' public holidays, a colour each. cal 2027 is the year.

Fuel consumption

8 l/100km mpg gives 29.4018 mpg. Litres per 100 km, miles per gallon, km per litre.

Clothes and shoes

44 it us gives the shoes and the clothes, men and women. Sizes change with the brand: Aequalis says so. In the app, the Sizes key shows the shoe and clothes keys.

Distances

Rome to Paris gives 1,107 km. Cities, countries or coordinates.

Trivia

For the curious: the live distance of the Voyager probes, the distance between the planets, how many satellites are in orbit right now. Open Trivia in the app and look.

Runs everywhere

Aequalis runs on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android. iOS is coming. One core, the same answers on every one of them.

How it works on Android

1. Type

Letters, digits and operators share one keyboard. The answer updates after every key.

2. Pick

Suggestions sit above the keys. When a request is ambiguous, you get one button per reading.

3. Keep

Press enter to keep it in the history. Copy or share the result with one tap.
