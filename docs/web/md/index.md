# Type it. Get it

Aequalis answers maths, units, currencies and time as you type.

Write your request the way you would say it.
If it can be read in more than one way, Aequalis asks which one you mean: no guessing.

Aequalis is available for Mac, Linux, Windows and Android (currently in the close testing phase)

## What you can type {.grid}

### Maths {.card}

`12+4/2` gives `14`, `2^10` gives `1024`, `20% of 150` gives `30`, and `sqrt`, `log`, `sin`, `5!` are there too.

### Units {.card}

`100 km/h mph` gives `62.1371 mph`. Length, area, volume, mass, temperature, speed, pressure, energy and more.

### Currencies {.card}

`12 usd euro` by code, name, symbol or country. Rates are synced once a day.

### Dates and time {.card}

`12 Sept 2026 + 5wd` gives `18/09/2026`. `10:00AM Paris to New York` gives the time over there.

### Fuel consumption {.card}

`8 l/100km mpg` gives `29.4018 mpg`. Litres per 100 km, miles per gallon, km per litre.

### Clothes and shoes {.card}

`44 it us` gives the shoes and the clothes, men and women. Sizes change with the brand: Aequalis says so. In the app, the Sizes key shows the shoe and clothes keys.

### Distances {.card}

`Rome to Paris` gives `1,107 km`. Cities, countries or coordinates.

### Trivia {.card}

For the curious: the live distance of the Voyager probes, the distance between the planets, how many satellites are in orbit right now. Open Trivia in the app and look.

## Runs everywhere {.grid}

Aequalis runs on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android. iOS is coming. One core, the same answers on every one of them.

## How it works on Android {.grid}

### 1. Type {.card}

Letters, digits and operators share one keyboard. The answer updates after every key.

### 2. Pick {.card}

Suggestions sit above the keys. When a request is ambiguous, you get one button per reading.

### 3. Keep {.card}

Press enter to keep it in the history. Copy or share the result with one tap.
