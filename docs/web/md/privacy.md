# Privacy policy

Aequalis for Android (`io.lucapf.aequalis`), by Luca Palanca Falsini. Effective 1 November 2026.

**In short:** Aequalis collects no personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. What you type stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your requests and their results.** The calculations happen on the phone. The history is kept in the app's private storage, so other apps can't read it.
- **Your settings and appearance.**
- **Text shared to Aequalis** (Share, the Quick Settings tile, the icon's shortcut) is worked out on the phone, like a request you type.
- **The clipboard** is read only when you tap Paste, and written only when you tap Copy.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app downloads

Aequalis downloads public data so that its answers stay current. Every download is a plain request for a fixed file or query, and none of them contains what you typed.

| Data | From |
|---|---|
| Exchange rates | ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) |
| Past days' rates, the city archive, public holidays | The Aequalis repository on GitHub (github.com) |
| The Trivia's live values (planets, spacecraft) | NASA JPL Horizons (ssd.jpl.nasa.gov) |
| The Trivia's satellites | CelesTrak (celestrak.org) |
| Wikipedia pages, when you open one from the Trivia | Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) |

Like any server on the internet, these services see your IP address and the standard details of the request (the address asked for, the time, the device's system). Their own privacy policies cover how they treat this. Wikipedia pages open in the app's built-in web viewer with JavaScript on, so Wikipedia's policy applies to them as it would in a browser.

You control the downloads in Settings:

- **`offline`:** no network at all. The data stays as last downloaded.
- **`night.sync`:** when switched on, the data is downloaded once a day at night, only on Wi-Fi.

## Backup

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android backs up your settings, history and appearance, and restores them on a new phone. Google stores the backup under your account's settings. The developer cannot see it. Downloaded data is never backed up.

## Permissions

Only **Internet**, for the downloads above. The app asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Deleting your data

Remove a single history entry with its ⋮ › Remove, or everything with History › Clear the history. Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Aequalis › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app holds on the phone.

## Children

Aequalis is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, children included.
